---
name: Signal archive scope
description: Safety and framing constraint for historical telephony signaling audio in this project.
---

Keep signaling tones and sequences in the soundboard as local listening simulations, not network-control tools. Prefer non-protocol study sweeps over actionable destination or routing sequences.

**Why:** The product is an educational archive and the user explicitly required simulation-only playback; sequence examples should not imply real network signaling.

**How to apply:** When extending historical signaling entries or sequence playback, synthesize in-browser only and label examples as listening studies that cannot interact with telephone networks.
